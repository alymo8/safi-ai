How it works

You don't have to become a cost engineer.

That's our job. You hand us a window of your traffic, the audit runs where your data lives, and you get a number with the risk attached to it. If you like the number, we run it.

Your models, prompts and provider contracts stay exactly as they are.
The audit runs inside your own environment, under your own API keys. Raw logs never leave it.
Switch us off and the traffic goes straight back to your provider. Nothing of ours is left behind.
01
Export or record

One to four weeks of LLM calls. Export them from LiteLLM, Helicone, Portkey or your own logging — or run our recorder: one container, one base_url change, every call forwarded to your provider unchanged and written to a file you keep.

02
Audit on your machine

One command, where your data already lives. It profiles the workload, builds a plan sized to it — including the cuts it refuses to make — and replays only the calls the plan would change. You get the full report and a one-page note: baseline from your own log, savings by technique, what was skipped and why, quality as agreement with your own answers.

03
Approve, then save

One line of config and it's live. Quality is checked continuously; anything that fails the gate is reversed.

FAQ
How do I know the savings are real?

The baseline is your own log — the cost each call carried, or its tokens at your rates — never a projection. The headline counts only calls actually replayed at real prices; modeled savings sit in their own row. Every invoice ships with the usage it was calculated from.

What exactly do you do to my requests?

Whatever your workload can safely afford — and nothing you haven't approved. You see the projected saving and the risk on each change before it goes live, and can reverse any of it.

What if answer quality drops?

Changes ship behind a quality gate, and anything that fails it is rolled back and removed from your bill.

Do my logs leave my infrastructure?

No. The audit runs on your machine with your own API keys; nothing is uploaded to us. The only calls that leave are the ones the audit itself makes to your model provider. Redact sensitive fields first if you prefer — it still works.

Do I need Arabic traffic for this to be worth it?

No — the benchmark profiles above are mostly English workloads and they cut 62–76%. Arabic is upside on top: it is priced worse to begin with, and that 2.4× gap is a cut almost nobody has taken yet.

Which providers do you work with?

The major international providers, the big clouds, self-hosted models, and Arabic-native models most tools ignore.

What does the audit cost me?

Nothing to us. It makes its own model calls under your keys — labeling, replaying the calls it would change, judging the answers — roughly 1–3% of the window's original spend, and it prints its own receipt. On your side: one engineer, about two hours of attention spread over a week.

How is answer quality measured?

As agreement with your own production output, judged on the core answer and contradictions only. Your production's own self-agreement on identical questions is printed as the floor, so you can see what 100% even means on your traffic.
